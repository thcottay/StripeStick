The problem

You can’t fix
what you can’t see.

Most swing faults happen where you can’t watch them: over your shoulder at the top, or in your hips through impact. You feel something’s off, and the ball confirms it. But in the moment, you’re guessing.

  • 01

    The fault

    High hands at the top.

    Your arms lift above your shoulder plane instead of turning with it. The result is a steep downswing and contact you can’t count on.

  • 02

    The fault

    Not enough hip turn.

    Your hips stall on the way back, so your arms do all the work. Power leaks out, and the club often comes over the top.

  • 03

    The fault

    Hips spinning out.

    Your hips open too early on the way down. The face stays open, and the ball slices or pulls.

Why other tools fall short

Most training aids stay put. Your swing doesn’t.

  1. Alignment sticks on the ground show where you’re aimed, not how you’re moving.
  2. Video shows the fault after the swing is over.
  3. Mirrors only work where there’s a mirror.
  4. Lessons help, but your coach doesn’t come home with you.

Fault by fault

One stick.
Multiple drills.

01

For high hands at the top.

Set the stick across your trail shoulder front to back, emulating Ben Hogan’s “pane of glass” concept. When your hands extend above the shoulder plane in your backswing, you’ll feel the stick.

02

For not enough hip turn.

Set the stick across your waist. It shows how far your hips rotate on the way back, so a stalled turn is easy to spot.

03

For hips spinning out.

Keep the stick at your waist and ensure your hands drop in front of the stick before you complete your turn.

Good to know

FAQ

Will it work with my shirt?

It’s designed for common golf shirts, from thin performance polos to heavier layers.

Is it safe?

The magnets are tuned to let go under excess force, and the wrist tether keeps a loose stick off the turf. Keep the magnets away from pacemakers and other implanted medical devices, and away from cards and phones.

Can I use it on the course?

It’s made for the range and practice swings. Using a training aid during a scored round breaks the Rules of Golf.

Where does the rail go?

At the top of your shoulder for Stripe 01, or at your belt line for Stripe 02 and 03. An optional elastic band or skin-safe strip holds it in place.

How big is it?

The stick is 36 in (91.4 cm) long, with a round carbon-fiber profile. The backing rail is 4.5 in (11.4 cm) long, 0.4 in (10 mm) wide and about 0.1 in (2–3 mm) thick.
